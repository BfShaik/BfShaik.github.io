About

The short version.

I am a Principal Cloud Architect at Oracle, where I have spent the last decade leading enterprise cloud strategy, multi-cloud migrations, and cloud-native application design for large scale ISVs and enterprises. Before Oracle, I spent fifteen years building production Java, J2EE, and .NET systems for organizations including Walmart Financial Services, Visa Global, MetLife, and Dun and Bradstreet, so the cloud architecture I design today is grounded in a long career of shipping and operating real software.

Over the past year that experience has turned toward hands on agentic AI work: building Model Context Protocol tooling, experimenting with agent frameworks, and figuring out how AI agents actually get deployed and operated on Oracle Cloud Infrastructure rather than just prototyped. This site is where that work lives in public, project by project, alongside the OCI architecture and migration work that is still my day to day.
